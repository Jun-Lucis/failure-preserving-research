# Experimental handoff

## The physical-data boundary

AI-assisted individual computational research can often reach:

- mathematical formulation;
- numerical implementation;
- broad parameter exploration;
- robustness and sensitivity tests;
- comparison with public databases;
- generation of explicit measurable predictions.

A hard limit appears when the decisive physical measurement is not publicly available and cannot be generated by the individual researcher.

## Public-data reach

In the BIG programme, public or published data permit partial external comparison in domains such as nuclear fission, fusion-related quantities, and anisotropic single-crystal silicon wet etching.

That reach is valuable but incomplete. Public datasets reflect measurements chosen for other purposes. They may not contain the exact parameter region, angular resolution, temporal resolution, boundary condition, control variable, or uncertainty characterization needed to discriminate a new model.

## Handoff object

A useful handoff to an experimental group should be more specific than an idea.

```text
model
+ code
+ competing hypotheses
+ retained negative results
+ frozen prediction
+ requested measurement
+ falsification criterion
```

The aim is to reduce the cost of deciding what experiment would be informative.

## Complementarity

The proposed division of labor is:

```text
individual + AI
    broad computational exploration
    hypothesis narrowing
    public-data comparison
    explicit prediction
          |
          v
laboratory / organization / domain experts
    sample preparation
    apparatus and calibration
    intervention
    new measurement
    independent replication
```

This is not a claim that either side is sufficient alone.
