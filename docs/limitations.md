# Limitations

This project should be read as a methodological case study, not as a universal theory of scientific productivity.

## Case-study limitation

The current evidence comes primarily from one independent research programme conducted by one researcher. It cannot establish population-level productivity effects or generalize quantitatively across disciplines.

## AI-model dependence

The practical depth, speed, and continuity of the workflow may depend strongly on the capability of the reasoning model used at a particular time. Subjective changes in research quality across model generations are relevant historical observations but are not controlled comparisons.

## External computation is not proof

Python/Colab execution can reveal contradictions between equations, code, and numerical outcomes. It does not establish that the modeled equations are physically correct, that the discretization converges, or that the interpretation is unique.

## Failure salvage can overfit history

A sufficiently flexible researcher can always invent a new story after a failure. For that reason, archived failure data used for redesign are treated as discovery data. New claims require fresh frozen or held-out tests when possible.

## Public-data selection

External comparison is limited by what other researchers have already measured and published. Available databases may omit exactly the variables or parameter ranges needed for decisive discrimination.

## Experimental boundary

An independent individual researcher generally cannot reproduce the capabilities of a laboratory, accelerator, fabrication line, microscope facility, or dedicated materials-characterization programme. New physical evidence may require organizations, equipment, safety systems, calibration expertise, and specialized tacit knowledge.

## Theory-method separation

A disciplined research process does not validate the underlying theory by itself. BIG may contain correct, incorrect, limited, or unresolved scientific claims while still serving as a useful case study of research process.

## Cost is reduced, not zero

Compute time, electricity, cloud resources, human verification, error diagnosis, archival work, and opportunity cost remain real. The claim is a shift in marginal cost and reusability, not the disappearance of cost.
