# Salvage type dictionary

The ledger uses the following working categories.

## COMPUTATIONAL_REUSE

Previously computed numerical assets are reused directly: trajectories, profiles, roots, gradients, caches, scans, or checkpoints.

## CONSTRAINT_REUSE

A negative result removes or weakens part of the hypothesis space and therefore changes the next question.

## MODEL_REDESIGN

The structure of a failed prediction motivates a new mathematical relation or model.

## STATE_ENRICHMENT

A failure indicates that the current state description may be missing information such as history, path, geometry, or another variable.

## EXPLANATORY_VARIABLE_CHANGE

The same observed pattern is recast using a different explanatory quantity rather than repeatedly narrowing one failed claim.

## DECOMPOSITION

A failed one-law description is replaced by a structured sum, factorization, or separation of contributions.

## PROTOCOL_REPAIR

A failed/invalid protocol exposes a design problem. The old outcome is retained and a new protocol is frozen independently.

## LIMIT_LOCALIZATION

A broad transfer or universality claim fails, but later tests identify a narrower region or transformation where structure survives.

## REPRESENTATIONAL_SALVAGE

A cluster of earlier failures becomes informative after a change of observable, coordinate, or conceptual representation.

This is the strongest form of research-memory reuse in the current case study.

## Classification rule

A salvage type describes how an earlier result influenced later research. It does not upgrade the earlier verdict.
