# Figure — Selected BIG failure-salvage lineages

![Selected BIG failure-salvage lineages](big_salvage_lineage_v0_1.svg)

**Caption.** Selected audited lineages from the BIG case study. Arrows mean that a retained result influenced or constrained a successor action; they do not mean that the parent verdict was upgraded. The examples span external transfer, predictor redesign, state/question enrichment, explanatory-variable change, representational salvage, protocol repair, and limit localization. Claim-bearing successor PASS results use separately identified fresh or held-out evaluations where the source record supports that distinction.

Source tables: `evidence/stage_lineage_B19_B40_v0_3.csv`, `evidence/failure_salvage_ledger_v0_2.csv`, and the case-study audit notes.
