# Figure — Lineage-audit sensitivity

![B19-B40 lineage-audit sensitivity](lineage_sensitivity_v0_1.svg)

**Caption.** Sensitivity of the explicit downstream backward-link fraction in the standardized B19-B40 audit. The primary named-stage broad dictionary gives 22/25 = 88.0%. A stricter FAIL/NOT_SUPPORTED-style dictionary gives 14/16 = 87.5%. Coarse B-family aggregation requiring every negative/limiting source in a family to be reused gives 13/16 = 81.25%. Excluding two explicit terminal/closeout sources from the broad denominator gives 22/23 = 95.65%. These bars answer different counting questions and are shown only to test sensitivity of the descriptive conclusion; they are not estimates of a universal failure-reuse rate.

Source data: `docs/quantitative_lineage_audit_v0_3.md` and `evidence/stage_lineage_B19_B40_v0_3.csv`.
